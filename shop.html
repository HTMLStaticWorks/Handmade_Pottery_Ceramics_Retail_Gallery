MATERIAL INTEGRITY

Sourced earth & slow processing

Understanding the stoneware, terracotta and raw glazes crafted in our studio.

01 · Clay Sourcing

Wild & Refined Clays

We blend natural stoneware with high-plasticity terracotta for optimal strength and rich, earthy warmth.

02 · Ash Glazes

Natural Mineral Sheen

Wood ash, feldspar, and iron oxide combine under high kiln heat to form unique tactile satin glazes.

03 · Slow Kiln Firing

Double Vitrification

Slow reduction firing cures every piece over 36 hours, ensuring complete water-tightness and longevity.
