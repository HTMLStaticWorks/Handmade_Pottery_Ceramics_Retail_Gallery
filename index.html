OUR CRAFT

Slow clay, high fire &
natural glazes.

Every ceramic vessel undergoes a multi-day journey from raw earth to finished form.

Step 01 · Shaping

Wheel Throwing & Trim

Hand-wedged stoneware and terracotta clay are centered on the wheel, thrown slowly, and trimmed with handmade tools.

Step 02 · Glazing

Ash & Mineral Glazes

We mix raw minerals, wood ash, and natural oxides to create rich, organic glazes with depth and natural tactile variance.

Step 03 · Firing

High Temperature Kiln

Fired to over 1220°C in small batches, locking in strength, water-tight durability, and one-of-a-kind surface character.
